Welcome to my blog. I'm Jake, and I'm on a journey into the intersection of security, governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) with AI systems.

## Why AI Security?

The AI landscape is evolving rapidly, and so are the security challenges that come with it. As organizations increasingly rely on AI service providers, the question isn't just "how do we build secure AI?" but "what controls do AI service providers need to maintain trust?"

This is where my focus lies: understanding the security and compliance requirements for organizations operating AI systems at scale.

## Building in Public

I believe in transparency and learning through sharing. This blog will document:

- **Technical deep-dives** into security architectures for AI systems
- **Compliance frameworks** and how they apply to AI service providers
- **Threat models** and risk assessments specific to AI/ML workflows
- **Tools and projects** I build along the way
